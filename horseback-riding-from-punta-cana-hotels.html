Horseback riding from Punta Cana hotels: pickup time and price

Two rides: sunset on the beach in Uvero Alto (4–6 PM) or the countryside and Macao Beach (about 2 hours). From the 5 hotels in Punta Cana it costs $75 per adult and pickup is confirmed on WhatsApp. Find your hotel below; we confirm the exact time on WhatsApp the day before.

How pickup works

Transport stops at the hotel entrance or lobby and waits 10 minutes. We confirm your exact time on WhatsApp the day before. Where a time says “confirmed on WhatsApp”, send us your hotel name and we will tell you before you pay.
