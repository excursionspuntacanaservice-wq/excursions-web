Party boat or snorkeling

Party boat or snorkeling in Punta Cana: which catamaran to pick

Both are about 3 hours on a catamaran off Bávaro, with hotel pickup and boats at 12:00 PM and 3:00 PM. The party boat ($65) is a hip hop party for adults 18+ with an open bar and a swim stop. Snorkeling ($75, kids 2 to 12 $37.50) is about the sea: snorkel on the reef with gear included and a natural pool, and the 12:00 PM boat takes the whole family.

Which one is for you?

  • Pick the party boat if everyone in your group is 18 or older and you want music, drinks and a swim: friends' trips, birthdays and bachelor or bachelorette groups.
  • Pick snorkeling if you want to see the reef and the natural pool, if it is your first time snorkeling (the crew helps everyone in the water) or if you travel with kids: book the 12:00 PM boat.
  • Mixed group? Adults can take the party boat one day and the whole family the snorkeling boat another day.

Frequently asked questions

Which is cheaper, the party boat or snorkeling?

The party boat is $65 per person. Snorkeling is $75 per adult and $37.50 for kids 2 to 12; under 2 go free.

Can I bring kids on either one?

Only on snorkeling, on the 12:00 PM boat. The party boat and the 3:00 PM snorkeling boat are for adults 18+ only.

Do both include drinks?

Yes. Both have an open bar on board. Premium and imported drinks are not included on the party boat.

Do both include hotel pickup?

Yes, both include pickup and drop-off at your hotel. We confirm your exact time on WhatsApp the day before.
