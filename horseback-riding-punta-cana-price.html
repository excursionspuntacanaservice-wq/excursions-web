Horseback riding in Punta Cana: price and which ride to choose

Horseback riding is $75 per adult, $37.50 per child aged 2 to 12 and free under 2. There are two rides: sunset on Uvero Alto beach, 4:00 to 6:00 PM, or Macao beach by day, about 2 hours, with 4 departures. Both include hotel pickup. No experience needed.

Frequently asked questions

How much is horseback riding in Punta Cana?

$75 per adult, $37.50 per child aged 2 to 12 and free under 2, with hotel pickup included.

Which should I pick, sunset or Macao?

The sunset ride is 4:00 to 6:00 PM on Uvero Alto beach, great for photos and couples. The Macao ride is about 2 hours through the countryside and Macao beach, with 4 departures a day.

Do I need experience?

No. The horses are calm and the guide goes at your group’s pace.

Can I cancel?

Yes, free up to 24 hours before with a full refund.
