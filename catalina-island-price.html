Catalina Island tour price from Punta Cana

The Catalina Island tour costs $75 per adult and $37.50 per child aged 5 to 8, with pickup at hotels in Punta Cana and Bávaro. Children 0 to 4 go free and from age 9 they pay the adult price. From Cap Cana and Uvero Alto it costs $85 per adult and $42.50 per child, with a minimum of 4 people.

Catalina or Saona?

Both cost the same from Punta Cana and Bávaro. Catalina is a small island off La Romana, quieter, with calm water and a reef close to the beach. Saona is the classic Caribbean postcard, with the natural pool, catamaran out and speedboat back. If you want the famous day, choose Saona; if you prefer calm and fewer people, choose Catalina.

If you are not staying at a hotel

If you are in an Airbnb, a villa or an apartment, we pick you up at the entrance or lobby of a nearby hotel on our route, or you go on your own to our meeting point: the Petromóvil gas station in Las Cañas del Este, across from Coco Bongo, Punta Cana. The price is the same. We confirm the spot and time on WhatsApp before you pay.

How payment works

You pay the full amount by credit or debit card on a secure Stripe page, in US dollars. Cards from other countries enabled for international purchases work. Your ticket arrives on WhatsApp and by email. We take no cash on the day of the tour. Free cancellation up to 24 hours before, with a full refund, and one free date change.
