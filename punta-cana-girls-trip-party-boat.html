Girls trip to Punta Cana: the hip hop party boat for your group

If you are planning a girls trip to Punta Cana, the hip hop party boat is a day out made for groups: $65 per person, an open bar of local drinks, hip hop, reggaeton and dembow on board, a stop to swim in shallow turquoise water and pickup at your hotel. It lasts about 3 hours, with boats at 12:00 PM and 3:00 PM. Everyone must be 18 or older.

How to plan it with your girls

  • One of you books and pays for everyone by card on Stripe's secure page. Up to 30 adults per booking, and the ticket arrives by email right after paying.
  • 12:00 PM or 3:00 PM? Same trip. The 12:00 PM boat leaves your evening free; the 3:00 PM boat lets you sleep in after a night out. Pickup is 10:15–11:40 AM or 1:15–2:40 PM depending on your hotel.
  • Staying in different hotels or in an Airbnb? Message us on WhatsApp before paying and we tell you where and when we pick each of you up.
  • Bring swimsuit, towel, sunscreen and a photo ID for everyone (18+ is checked).
  • Plans change? Free cancellation up to 24 hours before, with a full refund, or change the date once for free.
  • Drinks: the open bar is local drinks. Premium and imported drinks and tips are not included.

Frequently asked questions

What music do they play on the party boat?

Hip hop, reggaeton and dembow.

How much is the party boat for a girls trip?

$65 per person: 4 of you $260, 6 of you $390, 8 of you $520. Hotel pickup and the open bar of local drinks are included.

Is the party boat adults only?

Yes. Everyone must be 18 or older because of the open bar. Bring a photo ID.

Can we get picked up from an Airbnb?

Yes. Message us on WhatsApp before paying: we pick you up at a hotel lobby near your Airbnb or at our meeting point across from Coco Bongo.

Who answers if something comes up?

Carlos, the owner, and the local team answer on WhatsApp +1 829 962 1705, the same number before, during and after the trip.
