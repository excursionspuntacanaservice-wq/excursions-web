Overview

History, with a guide

Santo Domingo is a couple of hours from Punta Cana. We pick you up at your hotel, and with a guide you walk the Colonial Zone and visit the Catedral Primada de América, the first cathedral in the Americas, and the Alcázar de Colón. Then lunch and the ride back to your hotel.

Questions and policies

Guides: Santo Domingo tour price by area · how to book and pay from your country · tours with kids: price of every tour

How much is the Santo Domingo tour from Punta Cana?

$75 per adult and $37.50 per child aged 5 to 8, with pickup at hotels in Punta Cana and Bávaro, transport, guide and lunch. Children 0 to 4 go free and from age 9 they pay the adult price.

What do you visit?

The Colonial Zone on foot with a guide, the Catedral Primada de América and the Alcázar de Colón. Lunch is included.

How long is the drive from Punta Cana?

A couple of hours each way, so pickup is early and you are back in the afternoon.

How much is it from Cap Cana or Uvero Alto?

$85 per adult and $42.50 per child aged 5 to 8, with a minimum of 4 people.

I'm not staying at a hotel. Can you pick me up?

Yes. If you're at an Airbnb or a villa, we pick you up at the entrance or lobby of the closest hotel on our route, or you meet us at our meeting point. We agree on it with you before the tour.

How do I pay? Is it safe?

By card, on a secure Stripe checkout page. Stripe emails your receipt right away and we email your ticket right after. We don't take cash at pickup.

Can I cancel or change the date?

Cancel up to 24 hours before for a full refund. You can also move your date once, free, up to 24 hours before. Less than 24 hours before, there's no refund.

What if it rains?

Light rain doesn't stop the tour. If bad weather forces us to cancel on our side, you choose a new date or a full refund.
