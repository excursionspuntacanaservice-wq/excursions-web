Dune buggy · price

Dune buggy in Punta Cana: what your group pays

For buggies you pay per vehicle, not per person: single $80, double $90, VIP family buggy for 3 people $135 and for 4 people $180. It is about 4 hours on muddy trails in Macao, with a swim in Cueva Chiquita and a coffee, cacao and mamajuana tasting. Departures at 9 AM, 12 PM or 3 PM, with hotel pickup.

When you book you choose how your group splits up. If kids are coming, tell us their ages and we assign the right buggy.

How the day goes

  • Pickup at your hotel 7:30–8:30 AM for the 9 AM tour.
  • Ranch in Macao: safety briefing and ride on private mud trails.
  • Domitai Park: coffee, cacao, tea and mamajuana tasting.
  • Cueva Chiquita: swim in a crystal-clear cave.
  • Back to your hotel.

What is included

Your buggy, pickup and drop-off, guide and safety briefing, private trails, cave swim, tasting and water. Not included: photos and videos, tips, lockers or alcoholic drinks. You will get muddy: bring a swimsuit, towel, sneakers and clothes that can get dirty. Not suitable for pregnant women or people with reduced mobility.

Frequently asked questions

How much is a dune buggy in Punta Cana?

Single $80, double $90, VIP family buggy for 3 people $135 and for 4 people $180. The price is per buggy and includes hotel pickup.

Can kids ride?

Yes. Tell us their ages when booking and we assign the right buggy, for example the VIP family buggy for 3 or 4 people.

How long is it?

About 4 hours, with departures at 9 AM, 12 PM or 3 PM.

Buggy or ATV?

A buggy carries 1, 2, 3 or 4 people and is the family option. The ATV (quad) is single ($80) or double ($90) and the driver must be 18 or older.
