Dune buggy adventure in Punta Cana

Mud trails in Macao, a swim in a cave and a tasting of Dominican coffee and cacao.

  • About 4 hours · 9 AM, 12 PM or 3 PM
  • Hotel pickup included
  • Free cancellation up to 24 h

You drive your own buggy on private mud trails in the countryside of Macao. Then you stop at Domitai Park to taste coffee, cacao and mamajuana, and cool off in Cueva Chiquita, a cave with crystal-clear water. Choose a single, a double, or a family VIP buggy for 3 or 4 people.

What's included

  • Your buggy (single, double or family VIP)
  • Hotel pickup and drop-off
  • Professional guide and safety briefing
  • Private mud trails in Macao
  • Swim in the Cueva Chiquita cave
  • Tasting of coffee, cacao, tea and mamajuana
  • Drinking water

Not included

  • Photos and videos
  • Tips (optional)
  • Lockers
  • Alcoholic drinks

What to bring

  • Swimsuit and towel
  • Sneakers and clothes that can get muddy
  • Sunscreen and a hat
  • Cash for photos and souvenirs

Good to know

  • You will get muddy.
  • Coming with kids? Tell us their ages and we assign the right buggy.
  • Not suitable for pregnant women or people with mobility issues.

How the day goes

  1. PickupAt your hotel, 7:30–8:30 AM for the 9 AM tour
  2. 9:00 AMCheck-in and safety briefing at the ranch in Macao
  3. 9:15 AMOff-road ride on private mud trails
  4. 10:00 AMDomitai Park: coffee, cacao and mamajuana tasting
  5. 10:30 AMSwim in the Cueva Chiquita cave
  6. 11:15 AMFree time at a Dominican village shop
  7. 12:00 PMRide back to your hotel

The 12 PM and 3 PM tours follow the same plan, with pickups between 10:30 and 11:30 AM or 1:30 and 2:30 PM.

Questions and policies

How many people fit in one buggy?

A single buggy is for 1 person ($80), a double for 2 ($90), and the family VIP buggy takes 3 ($135) or 4 people ($180). In the booking box you choose how your group splits up: for example 3 people in one family VIP, or one double and one single.

I'm not staying at a hotel. Can you pick me up?

Yes. If you're at an Airbnb or a villa, we pick you up at the entrance or lobby of the closest hotel on our route, or you meet us at our meeting point. We agree on it with you before the tour.

How do I pay? Is it safe?

By card, on a secure Stripe checkout page. Stripe emails your receipt right away and we email your ticket right after. We don't take cash at pickup.

Can I cancel or change the date?

Cancel up to 24 hours before for a full refund. You can also move your date once, free, up to 24 hours before. Less than 24 hours before, there's no refund.

What if it rains?

Light rain doesn't stop the tour. If bad weather forces us to cancel on our side, you choose a new date or a full refund.
