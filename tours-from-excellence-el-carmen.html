How pickup works

Transport stops at the hotel entrance or lobby and waits 10 minutes. We confirm your exact time on WhatsApp the day before. Where a time says “confirmed on WhatsApp”, send us your hotel name and we will tell you before you pay.

Why $85 and a 4-guest minimum on the island tours

Uvero Alto is outside the regular bus route for Saona Island, Catalina and Santo Domingo, so pickup is direct at your hotel and the tour runs from 4 people. If you are fewer than 4, message us on WhatsApp before you pay.

Kids

Saona Island, Catalina and Santo Domingo: ages 0 to 4 free and 5 to 8 half price. Snorkeling and horseback riding: under 2 free and ages 2 to 12 half price. The party boat is for ages 18 and over only.

Frequently asked questions

Which tours pick up at Excellence El Carmen?

Saona Island, Catalina Island, the Santo Domingo tour, catamaran snorkeling, party boat, horseback riding and dune buggy. All include hotel pickup and drop-off.

What time is pickup at Excellence El Carmen for Saona Island?

Around 6:45 AM. The exact time is confirmed on WhatsApp the day before.

How much is Saona Island from Excellence El Carmen?

$85 per adult, with a minimum of 4 people. It includes pickup, catamaran, speedboat, lunch and an open bar of rum, soft drinks and water.

How do I pay and can I cancel?

By card on a Stripe page, in US dollars. Cancellation is free up to 24 hours before, with a full refund.
