Saona Island tour price from Punta Cana

The Saona Island tour costs $75 per adult and $37.50 per child aged 5 to 8, with pickup at hotels in Punta Cana and Bávaro. Children 0 to 4 go free and from age 9 they pay the adult price. From Cap Cana and Uvero Alto it costs $85 per adult and $42.50 per child, with a minimum of 4 people.

What the price includes

Hotel pickup and drop-off, catamaran out with music and open bar, speedboat back, the natural pool stop, Dominican buffet lunch, open bar with Dominican rum, soft drinks and water, and a guide.

Not included

Tips, beer, piña coladas and premium drinks.

Any extra fees?

No. The total you see before paying is what your card is charged, in US dollars.

Comparing prices

At hotel tour desks, the Saona Island tour usually costs around $120 per person. Before comparing, check three things: whether pickup in your area is included, what children pay and how late you can cancel.
