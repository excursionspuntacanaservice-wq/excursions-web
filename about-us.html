Is Excursions Punta Cana legit? Who we are and how to check

Excursions Punta Cana is the trade name of CASTILLO GLOBAL LLC, a company registered in Wyoming, United States, that sells Punta Cana tours directly on this website and on WhatsApp. We charge by card only through Stripe, we never ask for cash, and you can cancel free up to 24 hours before for a full refund. We are a small company and still have few published reviews, so here is exactly who we are and how to check us before you pay.

What we do and who runs the tour

We sell and coordinate tours: Saona Island, Catalina Island, snorkeling, horseback riding, Santo Domingo, dune buggy, ATV, party boat, swimming with dolphins and private boat. The tours are run by local operators in Punta Cana and Bayahibe that we work with. We are your contact before, during and after: the same WhatsApp confirms your pickup, answers on the day of the tour and handles any change.

How payment works

  • Credit or debit card only, in US dollars, on a secure Stripe page. We never see or store your card number.
  • We do not take cash or bank transfers, before or on the day of the tour.
  • Stripe emails your receipt right away.
  • Paying by card keeps your bank's purchase protection.

What happens after you pay

  1. You get your ticket by email from info@excursionspuntacana.net and on WhatsApp, with your booking reference.
  2. The day before, we confirm your exact hotel pickup time. You can see the estimated time for your hotel before you pay.
  3. On the day, you are picked up at your hotel entrance or lobby.

If you cancel or change the date

  • Free cancellation up to 24 hours before the tour, with a full refund to the same card.
  • Free date change up to 24 hours before, subject to availability.
  • If we cancel (for example, for bad weather), you choose a new date or a full refund.

The full policy is in cancellation and refunds and the terms of service.

How to check us before you pay

  1. Message us on WhatsApp and ask anything: your hotel's pickup time, what is included, what is not. If the answer does not convince you, do not pay.
  2. Look at the Google Maps listing and its reviews. There are only a few so far; we do not publish reviews we cannot back up.
  3. Read the cancellation policy and terms before booking.
  4. Check the payment page: it must be Stripe (checkout.stripe.com) and show the same total you saw on the website.

What we will not promise

The open bar on the Saona catamaran includes Dominican rum, soft drinks and water; beer and piña colada are not included. Tips are not included. We do not hold seats without payment: your booking is confirmed once we receive payment.
