Saona Island from Nickelodeon Punta Cana: pickup time and price

If you are staying at Nickelodeon Punta Cana, in Uvero Alto, we pick you up at the hotel around 6:20 – 6:45 AM and you are back around 6:00 PM. The price from your area is $85 per adult ($42.50 for kids 5 to 8), with a minimum of 4 people. You pay by card on Stripe and can cancel free up to 24 hours before.

How pickup works

The bus stops at the hotel entrance or lobby and waits 10 minutes. We confirm your exact time on WhatsApp the day before the tour. The time on this page is the approximate one for Uvero Alto.

Why $85 and a 4-guest minimum

Uvero Alto is outside the regular bus route, so pickup is direct at your hotel and the tour runs from 4 people. If you are fewer than 4, message us on WhatsApp and we will tell you your options before you pay.

What is included

Hotel pickup and drop-off, catamaran out with music and open bar (Dominican rum, soft drinks and water), speedboat back, natural pool stop, Dominican buffet lunch and guide. Not included: tips, beer, piña colada or premium drinks.
