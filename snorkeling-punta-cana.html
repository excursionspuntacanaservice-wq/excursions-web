Snorkeling catamaran in Punta Cana

Snorkel a reef, then stand in a turquoise natural pool. Open bar on board.

  • 3 hours · 12 PM family boat · 3 PM adults only
  • Hotel pickup included
  • Free cancellation up to 24 h

You sail from Bibijagua Beach in Bávaro on a catamaran with music and an open bar. First stop: a reef where the crew gets everyone in the water with snorkel gear, first-timers included. Second stop: a natural pool with waist-deep turquoise water. Then back to the beach and to your hotel.

What's included

  • Hotel pickup and drop-off
  • Snorkel gear
  • Open bar: beer, rum, sodas and water
  • Tropical fruit and snacks
  • Bilingual crew

Not included

  • Crew tips
  • Towels
  • Photo package

What to bring

  • Swimsuit
  • Towel
  • Biodegradable sunscreen

Good to know

  • 12:00 PM boat: for the whole family. 3:00 PM boat: adults 18+ only.
  • Kids 2–12 pay half; under 2 go free.

How the day goes

  1. PickupAt your hotel (time in the table below)
  2. DepartureBoard at Bibijagua Beach: the open bar and music start
  3. Stop 1Snorkeling on the reef, gear included
  4. Stop 2Natural pool with waist-deep water
  5. ReturnBack to Bibijagua and transfer to your hotel

About 3 hours on the water.

Questions and policies

Guides: price and times · tours with kids: price of every tour

Can kids come?

Yes, on the 12:00 PM family boat. The 3:00 PM boat is adults only (18+). Kids 2–12 pay $37.50; under 2 go free.

I'm not staying at a hotel. Can you pick me up?

Yes. If you're at an Airbnb or a villa, we pick you up at the entrance or lobby of the closest hotel on our route, or you meet us at our meeting point. We agree on it with you before the tour.

How do I pay? Is it safe?

By card, on a secure Stripe checkout page. Stripe emails your receipt right away and we email your ticket right after. We don't take cash at pickup.

Can I cancel or change the date?

Cancel up to 24 hours before for a full refund. You can also move your date once, free, up to 24 hours before. Less than 24 hours before, there's no refund.

What if it rains?

Light rain doesn't stop the tour. If the port closes for safety and the tour is cancelled on our side, you choose a new date or a full refund.
