Bachelor and bachelorette party boat in Punta Cana: what your group pays

For a bachelor or bachelorette party, a birthday or a friends' trip, the hip hop party boat is $65 per person with an open bar of local drinks, hip hop, reggaeton and dembow on board, a swim stop and hotel pickup. It lasts about 3 hours, there are boats at 12:00 PM and 3:00 PM, and everyone must be 18 or older.

How to book for the group

  • One person books for everyone: choose the date, the boat and the number of adults, and pay the total by card on Stripe's secure page. The form takes up to 30 adults per booking.
  • Everyone in the group must be 18 or older. Bring a photo ID.
  • Free cancellation up to 24 hours before, with a full refund. You can also change the date once, free, up to 24 hours before.
  • Staying in different hotels or in an Airbnb? Message us on WhatsApp before paying and we tell you where and when we pick you up.

Frequently asked questions

What music do they play on the party boat?

Hip hop, reggaeton and dembow.

How much is the party boat for a group?

$65 per person: 4 people $260, 6 people $390, 10 people $650. Hotel pickup and the open bar of local drinks are included.

Is there a minimum age?

Yes, 18. Everyone in the group must be 18 or older because of the open bar.

Can one person pay for the whole group?

Yes. One booking covers the whole group and is paid by card on Stripe's secure page. Your ticket arrives by email right after.

12:00 PM or 3:00 PM boat?

It is the same trip. Pick the time that suits your group when you book.
