Booking and payment

How to book and pay for your excursion before you travel

You can book from any country and months in advance. You pay by credit or debit card on a secure Stripe page, in US dollars. Your ticket arrives on WhatsApp and by email, and you can cancel free up to 24 hours before the tour.

Step by step

  1. Choose the tour, the date and how many people are going, on the website or on WhatsApp.
  2. See the exact total before paying. That total is what is charged.
  3. Pay by card on the Stripe page. If you book on WhatsApp, the payment link arrives on WhatsApp.
  4. Stripe emails your receipt right away. We send your ticket on WhatsApp and by email.
  5. The day before the tour we confirm your exact pickup time on WhatsApp.

Paying with a card from outside the US

Any credit or debit card enabled for international purchases works. The charge is in US dollars (USD) and your bank converts it to your currency. Depending on your country, your bank may add foreign-transaction fees; that is set by your bank. We add no fee to the published price.

If your bank declines the payment, a common reason is that international purchases are not turned on for the card. This is usually done in your bank app; then try again with the same link.

Why you pay in advance

Payment holds your spot. We do not take cash on the day of the tour. In return, you get free cancellation up to 24 hours before with a full refund, and one free date change.

Booking months ahead

You can book today for a date several months away. If your flight or dates change, you can move the tour date once at no cost, up to 24 hours before.

Frequently asked questions

Can I pay with a card from Canada or another country?

Yes, with any credit or debit card enabled for international purchases. The charge is in US dollars (USD) and your bank converts it. Your bank may add foreign-transaction fees; that is set by your bank, not by us.

Can I pay part now and the rest there?

No. Payment is in full when you book, with free cancellation up to 24 hours before the tour.

Can I pay in cash or by bank transfer?

No. Payment is by card only, on a secure Stripe page.

How long does a refund take if I cancel?

The refund goes back to the same card through Stripe. It usually takes 5 to 10 business days to show, depending on your bank.

How do I get the payment link and the ticket?

If you book on the website, you pay right there. If you book on WhatsApp, the payment link arrives on WhatsApp. Either way, your ticket arrives on WhatsApp and by email after payment.
