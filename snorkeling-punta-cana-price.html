Snorkeling in Punta Cana: price, times and what is included

Catamaran snorkeling is $75 per adult, $37.50 per child aged 2 to 12 and free under 2. It takes about 3 hours, leaves from Bibijagua beach in Bávaro and includes hotel pickup. There are two boats: the 12:00 PM boat is for the whole family and the 3:00 PM boat is adults only (18+).

How the trip goes

  1. Pickup at your hotel.
  2. Boarding at Bibijagua beach in Bávaro: music and open bar start.
  3. Stop 1: snorkeling on the reef, gear included. The crew gets everyone in the water, including first-timers.
  4. Stop 2: natural pool with waist-deep turquoise water.
  5. Back to Bibijagua and transfer to your hotel. About 3 hours at sea.

What is included

Hotel pickup and drop-off, snorkel gear, open bar on board, tropical fruit and snacks, and a bilingual crew. Not included: tips, towels or photo package. Bring a swimsuit, towel and reef-safe sunscreen.

Frequently asked questions

How much is snorkeling in Punta Cana?

$75 per adult, $37.50 per child aged 2 to 12 and free under 2. Includes hotel pickup, snorkel gear, open bar, fruit and snacks.

Can kids come?

Yes, on the 12:00 PM family boat. The 3:00 PM boat is adults only, 18+.

I have never snorkeled. Can I go?

Yes. We give you the gear and the crew gets everyone in the water, including first-timers.

How long is it?

About 3 hours at sea, plus the transfer from and to your hotel.

Can I cancel?

Yes, free up to 24 hours before with a full refund, or change the date once for free.

Ready to snorkel?

Pick your date and boat. With kids, book the 12 PM boat.
