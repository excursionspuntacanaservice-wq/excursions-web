Saona or Catalina Island? Which to choose

If you can only do one: choose Saona Island for the famous trip, with the starfish natural pool, catamaran out and speedboat back. Choose Catalina Island for a quieter day with calm water, reef close to the beach and fewer people. With us both are $75 per adult with hotel pickup included.

Choose Saona if…

  • It is your first time in Punta Cana and you want the famous trip.
  • You want to see the natural pool with starfish.
  • You like a catamaran with music out and a speedboat back.

Choose Catalina if…

  • You prefer a quieter day with fewer people.
  • You want more time in the water, with reef close to the beach.
  • You already did Saona on another trip.

Both cost the same, include hotel pickup and can be cancelled free up to 24 hours before. If you have the days, many people do both.

Frequently asked questions

Which is better, Saona or Catalina Island?

It depends on what you want. Saona is the classic Caribbean postcard, with the natural pool, catamaran out and speedboat back. Catalina is quieter, with calm water and reef close to the beach. If you can only do one and want the famous day, choose Saona; if you prefer calm and fewer people, choose Catalina.

Which one is cheaper?

They cost the same with us: $75 per adult and $37.50 per child aged 5 to 8. Kids 0 to 4 go free.

Which is better with kids?

Both are family trips. At Catalina the water is usually calm; at Saona the natural pool is shallow.

Do both include hotel pickup?

Yes. Both include pickup and drop-off at your hotel in Punta Cana, Bávaro, Cabeza de Toro and Macao. From Cap Cana and Uvero Alto they are $85 per adult with a minimum of 4 people.
