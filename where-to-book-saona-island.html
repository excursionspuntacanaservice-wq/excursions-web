Where to book Saona Island: hotel desk, booking website or direct?

There are three ways to book the Saona Island tour from Punta Cana: at your hotel tour desk, on a booking website, or direct with a local operator. At the hotel it is usually around $120 per adult; on booking websites we saw prices between $59 and $90; direct with us it is $75 per adult with pickup, lunch and open bar. Here is the comparison and what to ask before you pay.

5 questions to ask before paying for any Saona tour

  1. Is pickup from my hotel or area included, and at what time? With us, yes: find your hotel here.
  2. Catamaran, speedboat or both? With us, both: catamaran out and speedboat back.
  3. Which drinks are included? With us, Dominican rum, soft drinks and water. Beer and piña colada are not included.
  4. Is there any cash charge on the day? Not with us: the full total is paid by card when you book.
  5. What is the cancellation policy? With us, free up to 24 hours before with a full refund, plus one free date change.

How to book direct with us

  1. Choose your date and group size on the Saona Island page, or message us on WhatsApp.
  2. Pay by card on Stripe's secure page, in US dollars.
  3. You get your ticket by email and WhatsApp, and we confirm your pickup time the day before.
