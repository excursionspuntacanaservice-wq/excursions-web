Swim with dolphins in Punta Cana: price and which program to choose

You swim with dolphins at Dolphin Explorer, open every day, with hotel pickup. There are three programs: Funtastic $120 (40 minutes, no swimming needed), Explorer $155 (50 minutes, must be able to swim) and Excellence $199 (60 minutes with two dolphins). A companion who only watches pays $35.

Frequently asked questions

How much is swimming with dolphins in Punta Cana?

Funtastic $120, Explorer $155 and Excellence $199 per person, with hotel pickup. A companion who only watches pays $35.

Can kids swim with dolphins?

Yes. Funtastic needs no swimming and kids under 1 m go free with an adult. Explorer and Excellence require a minimum height of 1 m.

Which program should I pick?

Funtastic if you do not swim or have small kids; Explorer if you swim and want more time; Excellence for 60 minutes with two dolphins.

Can I cancel?

Yes, free up to 24 hours before with a full refund.
