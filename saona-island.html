Overview

The Caribbean island you’ve pictured

Saona is the island people picture when they think of the Caribbean: white sand, shallow turquoise water and almost no buildings. We pick you up at your hotel, drive about an hour to Bayahibe and you sail out on a catamaran with music and an open bar. You stop at the natural pool, spend a couple of hours on the beach with lunch, and ride back on a speedboat.

Is Saona Island worth it?

For most visitors, yes. It's the classic Caribbean day: a catamaran ride, the natural pool with starfish and a palm-lined beach with almost no buildings. It's a long day, with about an hour by bus each way to Bayahibe, so it suits people who want one full day out rather than a short trip.

How much does the Saona tour cost?

$75 per adult and $37.50 for children 5 to 8. Children 0 to 4 go free, and from age 9 they pay the adult price. The price includes hotel pickup, catamaran, speedboat, open bar and lunch.

How long is the day?

Pickup is between 6:20 and 8:10 AM depending on your hotel, and you're back around 6:00 PM.

Catamaran or speedboat?

Both. You go out on the catamaran with music and the open bar, and come back on the speedboat.

What is the natural pool?

A sandbank in the open sea with waist-deep water where starfish live. You can look and take photos, but please leave them in the water.

What is included?

Hotel pickup and drop-off, catamaran and speedboat, open bar with Dominican rum, soft drinks and water, Dominican buffet lunch, the natural pool stop and a guide. Tips, beer, piña coladas and premium drinks are not included.

What time is pickup at my hotel?

It depends on the area; the table on this page lists every zone. We confirm your exact time on WhatsApp the day before. The bus waits 10 minutes at each hotel.

I'm not staying at a hotel. Can you pick me up?

Yes. If you are staying in an Airbnb, a villa or an apartment, we pick you up at the entrance of the nearest hotel on our route or at our meeting point. Message us on WhatsApp with the name of the complex or the address and we confirm the exact spot and time before you pay.

What should I bring?

Swimsuit, towel, biodegradable sunscreen, a cap, a dry change of clothes and some cash if you want to tip.

Is it good for kids?

Yes. Children 5 to 8 pay half price, 0 to 4 go free and from age 9 they pay the adult price. The natural pool is shallow, and families do this trip every day. The Party Boat is the only tour for adults only.

Is there seaweed on Saona?

It changes with the season and the weather. If you're worried about a specific date, message us and we'll tell you what the boats are seeing that week.

Will I get seasick?

The catamaran is smooth. The speedboat back is faster and can be bumpy on windy days. If you get seasick easily, take your usual remedy before the trip. If you're pregnant, check with your doctor and message us first.

How do I pay? Is it safe?

By card, on a secure Stripe checkout page. Stripe emails your receipt right away and we email your ticket right after. We don't take cash at pickup.

Can I cancel or change the date?

Cancel up to 24 hours before for a full refund. You can also move your date once, free, up to 24 hours before. Less than 24 hours before, there's no refund.

Why book directly with you?

You pay $75 per adult with hotel pickup, lunch and open bar included, and you see your pickup time before you pay. The same WhatsApp number answers before, during and after the tour, and you can cancel free up to 24 hours before.

Should I book in advance?

Yes. Buses are assigned by hotel route and fill up on weekends and holidays. Booking two or three days ahead keeps your date, and you can still cancel free up to 24 hours before.

Do you speak English?

Yes. We answer WhatsApp in English and Spanish, and your ticket and pickup confirmation arrive in your language.

Will anyone try to sell me things?

Not from us: $75 is everything we charge, and we take no cash at pickup. On the island there are independent vendors and photographers, as on any Caribbean beach. A polite no is enough.

Are there extra fees at checkout?

No. The total you see on the button is what your card is charged. Stripe emails the receipt at once.

Can I pay with a card from outside the US?

Yes, with any credit or debit card enabled for international purchases. The charge is made in US dollars (USD) and your bank converts it to your currency. Depending on your country, your bank may add taxes or foreign-transaction fees; that is set by your bank, not by us. We add no fee to the price.

Can I book months in advance?

Yes. You can book today for a date several months away, and free cancellation up to 24 hours before the tour still applies.

How do I get the payment link and the ticket?

If you book on this page, you pay right here. If you book on WhatsApp, the payment link arrives on WhatsApp. Either way, your ticket arrives by WhatsApp and by email after payment.

What if my flight or travel dates change?

You can change the tour date once, free, up to 24 hours before. If you can no longer go, cancel up to 24 hours before and you get a full refund.

What if it rains?

Light rain doesn't stop the tour. If the port closes for safety and the tour is cancelled on our side, you choose a new date or a full refund.
