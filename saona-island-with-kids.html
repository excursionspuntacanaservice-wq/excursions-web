Saona Island with kids: what they pay and what to know

Yes, Saona Island is a family trip. Kids 0 to 4 go free, kids 5 to 8 pay $37.50 and from age 9 they pay the adult price ($75). The price includes hotel pickup, catamaran, speedboat, lunch and drinks. Below are exact totals for a few typical families.

What the day is like with kids

  • It is a long day. Pickup is between 6:20 and 8:10 AM depending on your hotel, and you are back around 6:00 PM. The bus ride to Bayahibe is about one hour each way.
  • The boats. You go out by catamaran, which is smooth. You come back by speedboat, which can bounce on windy days.
  • The natural pool. A sandbar in open sea with water about waist-deep on an adult, where starfish live. Kids can look at them and take photos, but they stay in the water.
  • The beach. Dominican buffet lunch, palm shade and a couple of hours to swim and relax.
  • Drinks. Soft drinks and water are on board along with rum. Beer and piña colada are not included.

What to pack for the kids

  • Swimsuit on from the hotel and dry clothes for the ride back.
  • Towel, hat and reef-safe sunscreen.
  • If your child gets seasick, whatever their pediatrician recommends, taken before the speedboat.
  • Some cash if you want to tip.

If you have a question about your child (age, seasickness, not a swimmer yet), message us on WhatsApp before you pay and we will answer for your date.

Frequently asked questions

Do babies pay for the Saona Island tour?

No. Children 0 to 4 go free.

From what age do kids pay the adult price?

From age 9 they pay the adult price: $75 from Punta Cana and Bávaro, or $85 from Cap Cana and Uvero Alto.

How much does a child aged 5 to 8 pay?

$37.50 from Punta Cana, Bávaro, Cabeza de Toro and Macao, and $42.50 from Cap Cana and Uvero Alto.

Is Saona Island good for young kids?

Yes. The natural pool is shallow and families do this trip every day. Keep in mind it is a full day: early pickup and back around 6:00 PM.

Is any tour adults only?

The Party Boat is the only adults-only tour. Saona Island, Catalina Island and the Santo Domingo tour are for the whole family.

Can I cancel if my child gets sick?

Yes. You can cancel free up to 24 hours before for a full refund, or change the date once, free, up to 24 hours before.
