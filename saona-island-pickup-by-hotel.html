Hotel not on the list?

Send us your hotel name on WhatsApp and we will tell you your time and price before you pay. If you are staying in an Airbnb, villa or apartment, we pick you up at a nearby hotel on the route or at our meeting point: the Petromóvil gas station in Las Cañas del Este, across from Coco Bongo.

What is included

Hotel pickup and drop-off, catamaran out with music and open bar (Dominican rum, soft drinks and water), speedboat back, natural pool stop, Dominican buffet lunch and guide. Not included: tips, beer, piña colada or premium drinks. You are back around 6:00 PM.

Frequently asked questions

What time is pickup for Saona Island?

Between 6:20 and 8:10 AM depending on your hotel: Uvero Alto from 6:20, Macao 6:45, Bávaro between 7:05 and 8:00, Cabeza de Toro 7:30 to 8:10, Punta Cana 7:45 to 8:00 and Cap Cana 7:30. We confirm your exact time on WhatsApp the day before.

How long does the bus wait at my hotel?

The bus waits 10 minutes at each hotel. Be at the lobby or entrance 5 minutes early.

Why is it $85 from Cap Cana and Uvero Alto?

Because the transfer from those areas is longer. The price there is $85 per adult and $42.50 per child aged 5 to 8, with a minimum of 4 people.

What time do I get back to the hotel?

Around 6:00 PM.
